Your morning, on your own

Everything before lunch is close together in Toyosu and Tsukiji. Your auction number and your teamLab passes are below. Show this page if anyone asks.

Tuna auction
Check-in 5:30 at the PR corner, Block 7, 3rd floor. Reception number 53241. The auction runs until about 6:25.
teamLab Planets
Two Premium Passes, already paid. Enter any time from 8:00 to 21:00 today. Skip the line and show the QR code at the Information Center.
Meet Yuuki
12:00 at Ginza Sushimasa. Address and directions are at the bottom of this page.
  1. 1
    Tuna auction deckCheck in at 5:30 with reception number 53241. You watch the auction through the window on the 3rd floor of Block 7.
  2. 2
    Breakfast at the marketSushi and seafood bowls in the restaurant area of the market. Many places open at 5:00.
  3. 3
    teamLab PlanetsOne stop on the Yurikamome line (Shijo-mae to Shin-toyosu), or a 5 minute taxi. Show the QR code at the Information Center; no need to queue. You walk barefoot inside and one room has knee-deep water.

    Tsukiji Outer MarketAbout 15 minutes by taxi from teamLab. Tamagoyaki, grilled seafood and knives. From here, Sushimasa is a 10 minute walk.

If you are tired after the auction, skip teamLab, rest at the hotel and come straight to lunch at 12:00. The teamLab passes stay valid until 21:00 today.

Toyosu, Tsukiji, then Ginza on foot

Auction at 5:30, lunch at 12:00, done about 15:30

The early part is all near the market and you do it at your own pace. After lunch everything is in Ginza, flat and on foot, and it ends a short walk from your hotel.
